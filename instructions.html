Your Exam Structure

Exam Rules

  1. Time limit: You have 120 minutes to complete the entire exam. The timer starts as soon as you click "Start Exam" and runs continuously.
  2. Total questions: 180 questions across 4 subjects.
    • Use of English: 60 questions
    • Each elective: 40 questions
  3. Marks: Your exam is scored out of 400 marks. Each subject is worth 100 marks.
  4. No negative marking: Wrong answers and unanswered questions carry 0 marks. You are not penalized for guessing — attempt every question.
  5. Free navigation: You can move between questions freely using the question grid. You can also flag questions to review later.
  6. Auto-save: Every answer is saved automatically. If your browser closes or you refresh, you can resume from where you left off.
  7. Timer alerts: You'll see warning colors at 30 min, 10 min, and 5 min remaining.
  8. Auto-submit: When the timer reaches 0:00, your exam is submitted automatically.
  9. No pausing: Once you start, the timer keeps running. If you close the browser, the timer continues.
  10. Work offline: No internet needed. Your answers are stored locally on this device.
